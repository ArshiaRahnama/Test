Permission note: any command marked (ADMIN) requires
xPlayer.permission_level > Config.CommandPerm (default: 10).
If you get "You Don't Have Permissions!", your permission group
needs adjusting in Config.lua or your ACE/permission setup.

⚠️ REQUIRED DEPENDENCY: this resource now uses ox_lib for all
notifications and ox_target for Academy entry/exit interactions.
Make sure both are installed and started BEFORE this resource in
your server.cfg:
  ensure ox_lib
  ensure ox_target
  ensure Unique_Capture

⚠️ REQUIRED SETUP BEFORE GOING LIVE: Config.AcademyEntryPoints in
Config.lua is a placeholder (a single fake coordinate). You must
replace it with real, safe locations on your map before enabling
the Training Academy, or players won't be able to enter it at all.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  1. THE DASHBOARD — TELL YOUR PLAYERS ABOUT THIS ONE
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

/capture
  Opens a full-screen dashboard with EVERYTHING in one place — no
  need to remember /captureStats, /captureMedals, /captureStandings
  separately. Shows: rank badge, kills/deaths/K-D/gang points/
  top-5 count/score, best zones, all-time Top 10 killers, this
  season's gang standings, countdown to next season reset, and
  your scarce medal collection with a live supply bar. Close with
  the X button or Escape.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  2. RUNNING A ROUND (core commands)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

/startCap                              (ADMIN)
  Starts a new capture round using the zones and time currently
  set. Announces to the WHOLE server that the round has started
  and tells everyone to use /joinCap. Does nothing if a round is
  already active.

/editCap                               (ADMIN)
  Opens the admin menu in-game: set zones, set round time,
  start the round, or send a server announcement — all from a
  menu instead of typing commands.

/endCap                                (ADMIN)
  Force-ends the current round immediately. Same result as the
  round timer reaching zero: results get saved, leaderboards get
  logged, everyone is pulled out of the capture world.

/joinCap
  Any player uses this to join the currently active round (must
  be in their gang first).

/leaveCap
  Lets a player leave the capture world at any time — whether
  they're mid-round, stuck in a menu, or anything in between.
  ⚠️ If a player is actively standing in a zone's marker
  (defending) when they use this, they get locked out of
  re-entering that SPECIFIC zone for a few minutes
  (Config.LeaveZonePenaltySeconds, default 180s) — this only
  triggers on a real /leaveCap, never when a round ends normally.

/reCap
  Self-service respawn — lets a stuck-but-alive player inside
  the capture world force a respawn without waiting for a real
  death.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  3. STATS & HISTORY (open to everyone unless noted)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

/captureStats
  Shows your own all-time Kills, Deaths, K/D ratio, how many
  times you've placed in a round's Top 5, your best zones, and
  the server's all-time Top 10 killers.

/captureHistory                        (ADMIN)
  Opens a menu listing the last 15 rounds — date, winning gang,
  top killer. Click any round for full Top 5 Killers/Gangs
  detail from that specific round.

/captureSeasons
  Lists the last 10 completed seasons: winning player + winning
  gang for each one.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  4. SEASONS
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

How it works automatically:
  Every player's all-time stats (Config: capture_player_stats)
  and every gang's season points (capture_gang_stats) reset on
  their own every Config.SeasonAutoResetDays days (default 30).
  Right before the reset:
    • A full raw JSON backup is saved to
      resources/Unique_Capture/backups/ (separate safety net,
      independent of the database archive).
    • Full standings (with final rank) are archived into
      capture_season_archive / capture_gang_season_archive —
      query these anytime for full season history.
    • Winners (player + gang) are recorded in capture_seasons
      and announced to the whole server.
  Prizes are NOT paid automatically — check capture_seasons /
  capture_gang_season_archive and hand them out yourselves.

/captureSeasonReset                    (ADMIN)
  Forces a season to end right now instead of waiting for the
  automatic timer. Use this if you want to run a season shorter
  than 30 days, or wrap one up for a special event.

If Config.EnableSeasonWarning is on (default: yes), the whole
server gets a heads-up chat message Config.SeasonWarningHoursBefore
hours (default 24) before an automatic reset happens — not just
at the moment it happens.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  5. VISUAL THEMES (purely cosmetic, zero gameplay effect)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

/captureTheme [name]                   (ADMIN)
  Changes the zone marker colors live for the whole server.
  Run it with no name to see the list of available themes.
  Built-in themes: Default, Halloween, Christmas, Bloodmoon.
  Add your own in Config.Themes.
  Anyone who joins mid-event automatically gets the active theme.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  6. DIAGNOSTICS / MAINTENANCE TOOLS
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

/captureDryRun                         (ADMIN)
  Checks your zones, round time, active theme, score weights,
  and database table/column names are all valid — WITHOUT
  actually starting a round. Run this after you change Config.lua
  to catch typos before players notice.

/captureHealth                         (ADMIN)
  Quick status report: is a round active, current theme, how many
  zones are configured, and a live row-count for every stat/
  history table. Good first step when something looks broken.

Admin commands have a short cooldown per-admin (5 seconds by
default, Config.AdminCommandCooldown) to stop accidental double-
presses/spam. Turn off with Config.EnableAdminRateLimit = false.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  7. ZONES FROM A FILE (optional, off by default)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

By default zones live in Config.DefaultZones. If you'd rather
manage them from a separate file:

  1. Set Config.EnableExternalZonesFile = true in Config.lua.
  2. /captureExportZones (ADMIN) — saves your CURRENT zones to
     resources/Unique_Capture/zones.json as a starting point.
  3. Edit zones.json by hand (or hand it to a builder).
  4. /captureImportZones (ADMIN) — reloads zones from that file
     and replaces the active ones. Blocked while a round is
     active, for safety — end the round first.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  8. LEAGUE MODE (optional, OFF by default)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Turn on with Config.EnableLeagueMode = true for a football-style
layer on top of the normal season system:

/captureStandings
  Live current-season gang points table, open to everyone.

Comeback Of The Season
  Fully automatic — announced to the server at season end,
  comparing each gang's final rank to last season's rank.

Playoffs (Config.EnablePlayoffs, its own separate toggle):
  At season end, the top 4 gangs by points are automatically
  paired into semifinals (1st vs 4th, 2nd vs 3rd) and announced.
  There's no automated 1-gang-vs-1-gang match mode, so admins
  referee each match manually and record the result:

    /capturePlayoffResult semi1 [gang_name]
    /capturePlayoffResult semi2 [gang_name]
    /capturePlayoffResult final [gang_name]

  The Final's matchup is filled in automatically the moment both
  semifinal winners have been recorded.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  9. PUBLIC READ-ONLY API (optional, OFF by default)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Config.EnablePublicAPI = true exposes a GET endpoint
(Config.PublicAPIPath, default "/uniquecapture/status") on your
server's game port, returning live JSON: round active/time
remaining, zone count, active theme, Top Killers, Top Gangs.

⚠️ IMPORTANT: FiveM only allows ONE HTTP handler per server.
Enabling this WILL override any other resource on your server
that also uses SetHttpHandler. Only turn this on if you're sure
nothing else does.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  10. SCARCITY ENGINE — LIMITED NUMBERED MEDALS (on by default)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

The moment a player's rank first crosses into "Legend" during a
season, they get a shot at a numbered medal from that season's
fixed pool (Config.ScarceMedalSupplyPerSeason, default 100).
Once the pool is empty, NOBODY else gets one that season — no
exceptions, no refills, even if they earned it. Serial numbers
are permanent, recorded forever in capture_scarce_medals, and
never reused.

/captureMedals
  Shows how many medals have been claimed this season and how
  many are left.

/mymedals
  Shows every medal you personally own, across every season.

Turn off entirely with Config.EnableScarcityEngine = false.
Change the pool size with Config.ScarceMedalSupplyPerSeason, or
which rank tier triggers it with Config.ScarceMedalRank.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  11. HALL OF FAME (on by default, fully automatic)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Any player who reaches "Legend" rank and then goes inactive for
Config.HallOfFameInactivityDays (default 60 days) gets
automatically, permanently inducted — no score attached, never
removed, even if they come back and play again later. It's purely
an honor, checked every 6 hours in the background.

/hallOfFame
  Lists the last 20 inductees with career kills and final rank.

Turn off with Config.EnableHallOfFame = false.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  12. TRAINING ACADEMY (on by default — but read the warning below)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

/captureAcademy
  Target-interact (ox_target) with the instructor NPC standing at
  one of Config.AcademyEntryPoints to enter — no more "press E"
  prompt. Teleports you into a SEPARATE routing bucket/world
  (Config.AcademyWorld, default 51) and spawns hostile NPCs,
  created server-side and networked to you, to fight. Never
  touches real kills, points, or any leaderboard table.

  ⚠️ Config.AcademyEntryPoints now defaults to a real spot (outside
  Mission Row PD) so it works out of the box — but it's still just
  an example. Change it to wherever fits your server.

  Each entry point also gets its own map blip (Config.AcademyBlip —
  sprite, color, scale) so players can actually see where to go
  instead of needing to be told the coordinates.

  The moment you walk in, you land inside a Safe Zone centered
  exactly on your spawn point (Config.AcademySafeZoneRadius, default
  8.0 units). Inside it you're genuinely untouchable — you're made
  invincible AND every NPC is switched to a neutral relationship
  group and stood down, so nothing can re-target you by accident.
  Step outside that radius and every NPC in the academy switches to
  hunting you immediately; step back in and they immediately back
  off again, with a heads-up notification either way. There's no
  on-screen marker for it (removed — it's a fixed, known radius
  around your spawn point, not something that needs a visual).
  (Config.AcademySafeZoneOffset lets you shift the safe zone
  off-center if you ever want the spawn and the safe area to be two
  different spots.)

  NPC count now ramps up instead of swarming you from second one:
  you start with just Config.AcademyStartNPCCount (default 1) NPC
  actively hunting you, and gain one more every
  Config.AcademyNPCCountStep total kills (default every 8), capped
  at Config.AcademyMaxNPCCount (default 4) so it never spirals out
  of control. New NPCs trickle in one at a time with a short random
  delay between them rather than all appearing at once. Accuracy and
  armor still scale up separately via Config.AcademyDifficultyStep.

  NPCs can hurt you but can never actually kill you. Your max health
  is boosted to Config.AcademyPlayerMaxHealth (default 500, restored
  to normal the moment you leave) and clamped so it can never drop
  below Config.AcademyHealthFloorPercent of that (default 12%). This
  is enforced the instant damage lands — not on a delay — so even a
  full rifle burst can't slip through and drop you. This is a
  sandbox, not a death trap. If you do somehow go down another way,
  you get an on-screen "Press E to respawn" prompt and revive right
  there through this server's real revive trigger
  (Config.ReviveTrigger) — dying does NOT kick you out of the
  Academy.

  A second instructor NPC spawns inside the Academy itself — target
  it (or use the dashboard's Leave button / /leaveAcademy) to exit.
  You're teleported back to exactly where you entered from.

  NPCs get tougher (accuracy + armor) as your permanent Academy
  kill count climbs — see Config.AcademyDifficultyStep /
  Config.AcademyMaxAccuracy / Config.AcademyMaxArmor.

  A marked Safe Zone inside the Academy makes NPCs stop attacking
  the instant you step into it (Config.AcademySafeZoneOffset/
  Radius) — useful to catch your breath.

  If you stay past Config.AcademyTimeLimitMinutes (default 20), you
  get a friendly reminder to go try a real round, repeating every
  Config.AcademyReminderIntervalMinutes.

  The first time you enter, a full step-by-step chat guide explains
  exactly how a real capture round works, and a green practice
  marker inside the Academy shows what a real capture point looks
  like.

/leaveAcademy
  Exits the academy and cleans up the NPCs.

While inside, a dedicated on-screen panel shows a live session
timer and your TOTAL Academy kill count (a separate, permanent
counter in capture_academy_stats — it never resets and can never
touch your real capture stats, rank, or medals). Killed NPCs
respawn automatically so training never runs dry. Your badges
(Sharpshooter/Marksman Elite/Academy Legend) and the all-time
Academy leaderboard are visible right in the Academy tab of
/capture.

⚠️ REQUIRED SETUP: Config.AcademyCoord is a placeholder
(vector3(0.0, 0.0, 72.0)) and needs to be changed to a real, safe,
out-of-the-way spot on your map before you rely on this — otherwise
players will spawn at 0,0,72 which is not a real location.

Turn off with Config.EnableAcademy = false.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  13. FULL DISCORD LOGGING (fully configurable)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Config.Webhooks in Config.lua has one slot per category:

  RoundStart    A round starts
  RoundEnd      A round ends (short summary)
  ZoneCapture   A zone changes hands mid-round
  Kills         Every individual kill (gets noisy - that's the point)
  Season        Season warnings, season end, Hall of Fame inductions
  Medals        Every scarce medal claimed
  Admin         Theme changes, forced resets, manual start/end,
                dry-run results, zone import/export

Leave any category as "" to turn OFF just that one. Point two or
more categories at the same webhook URL to combine them into one
Discord channel — totally up to you how granular you want it.

Master switch: Config.EnableDetailedLogging = false turns
everything off at once, regardless of individual URLs.

A missing or broken webhook NEVER breaks gameplay — at worst it
prints a one-line warning to console.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  14. SPECTATOR MODE (on by default — now permission-gated)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

⚠️ REQUIRES PERMISSION: xPlayer.permission_level must be ABOVE
Config.SpectatePermLevel (defaults to Config.CommandPerm, the same
threshold as the other admin commands) to run /captureSpectate at
all. Set Config.SpectatePermLevel = 0 in Config.lua if you want to
open spectating back up to every player.

/captureSpectate
  Only works while a round is active. Drops you into a free-flying
  camera over the battle — WASD to move, mouse to look, Space/Ctrl
  for up/down, hold Shift to move faster. Your character is frozen,
  invisible, and invincible for the entire time — you cannot be
  seen, hurt, or interfere with the round in any way, and you don't
  need to be in a gang to use it.

  Press TAB at any time to open a live list of every player
  currently in the round. Click a name to lock the camera onto
  them — it orbits around that player instead of flying freely
  (mouse still rotates the view), and a Health/Armor panel tracks
  them live. Hit "Free Camera" in the list (or press TAB again) to
  unlock and go back to flying wherever you like. If the player you
  locked onto disconnects or leaves the capture world, you're
  automatically dropped back to free camera with a notification —
  spectating never gets stuck watching nobody.

/leaveSpectate (or press ESC)
  Exits and puts you back exactly where you were standing.

Automatically exits cleanly if the round ends while you're
watching. Turn off with Config.EnableSpectate = false.
